# Native plats (added 2026-09-30)

Both sheets come from the surveyor's `Boulev.dwg`, the same drawing lineage as `cad/Boulev_CLEAN.dxf`. Each is 36 × 24 in at 1" = 30'. The copies here are byte-identical to the originals on Drive, in `00 OTB/Belle Realty SOT Documents/Files and Docs to Place/Plats  Survey/`.

## `plat-of-survey-detailed-2019.pdf`

- **What it is:** ALTA/ACSM survey for Belle Realty of Lafayette, LLC, by Montagnet & Domingue, Inc. A. E. Montagnet, P.L.S. 4484. Dated 5/20/1994, last revised 7/19/2019 (rev 10: "updated tenants, zoning, flood map").
- **What it governs:** boundary and legal description, the parking-variance note (Req'd 344 / 324 provided), title exceptions, the block dimension strings (80.8' LOT 12, 108.00' end block, depths 85.45' / 84.5'), and the **building height labels**: 16.4 typical · 13.2 at 105 · 20.6 at 103 · 23.6 at 101 · BLD. HT. 13.5 at the 101 end projection.
- **General note:** "BUILDING HEIGHT EXCLUDES FACADE WHICH IS APPROX. 23.6'".

## `site-plan-simple-2020.pdf`

- **What it is:** Site plan for Belle Realty of Lafayette, LLC. Plot stamp Sep 29 2020.
- **What it governs:** the **per-unit frontage for every suite**, including 101 (83.0), 103 (25.0), 109 (30), 111 (20) and 139–145 (23.1).
- **Known label error:** the plan prints 113 as 32.3'. The CAD demising lines measure 30.79', and 30.8' closes the survey's 80.8' block, so 30.8 is used (A-1 REV 15).
