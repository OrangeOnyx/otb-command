## Operating terms (2020 agreement)

- **§1: JD Bank grants to Belle.**
  - **What:** 13 JD Bank parking spaces, marked "X" on Exhibit B-1, plus ingress/egress for that parking.
  - **Where on A-1:** the `jdbank` parking zone (6 + 7).
  - **Term:** expires midnight, December 30, 2034.
- **§2: Belle grants to JD Bank.**
  - **What:** the "Belle Servitude Area" on Exhibit A-1. It has two parts: the Right of Passage ("///") and the Belle Parking Spaces ("=").
  - **Payment:** monthly payments to Belle, due on the 1st.
  - **Term:** expires 12/30/2034.
  - **Belle may cancel on either of these events:**
    - (i) JD Bank's use changes, i.e. it is no longer a bank branch.
    - (ii) JD Bank sells all or part of its property.
- **§5: Maintenance.** Each party keeps its easement area clear. Maintenance of the Right of Passage is shared 50/50.
- **§7: Insurance.** Each party carries CGL insurance and names the other as additional insured.
- **§10: Indemnity.** JD Bank indemnifies Belle for use of the Belle Servitude Area.
