# OTB Visual Library — round 2 (2026-10-01)

These images are for presentation only. They are AI-generated, so never use them as a source for counts, dimensions or tenancy. The geometry lives in A-1, A-3 and A-8. `manifest.json` lists every image with its Higgsfield job ID and URL.

## What's in the set
- **Six site-element plates**, in the master-board illustration style:
  - trees
  - landscape islands
  - ADA and standard stalls on concrete
  - the dumpster enclosure
  - the 14-panel pylon, with its panels left blank so the app can add tenant names
  - the electrical row, rerun with no labels
- **23 storefronts**, one per tenant, covering all 27 suites. Tenants with two suites share one image: 101/103, 115/117, 125/127 and 139/141. The vacant suites 131 and 133 have blank sign bands for leasing flyers, and 135B is the Belle office.
- **The master board** (4800 × 3060). I laid it out with Pillow in the Higgsfield sandbox from the real images above, with no AI step. It has:
  - a header band in the brand palette, with the property figures
  - the golden-hour center image and the pylon
  - the 23 storefronts
  - all eight site plates: two from round 1, six from this round
  - a Cypress Command Platform footer

## Caveats
- **Sign lettering is a simple sans-serif channel letter, not any tenant's real logo.** Check each tenant's trademark and sign approval before any external use.
- **Frontage widths were in the prompts, but the model does not hold proportions exactly.** These images are not to scale.
- **None of this is in the repo or on Drive yet.** The cloud session's network blocks Higgsfield's upload and CDN hosts. The images live in the Higgsfield library, and the manifest URLs open in a browser.
