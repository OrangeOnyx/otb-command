## Before leaving

- [ ] Charge camera, phone/tablet and power bank; clear adequate storage without deleting unbacked-up captures. Clean camera lenses and phone sensors.
- [ ] Set both devices to the correct local date/time; record the time zone. Write down the camera/device models, scan app/version and selected capture mode.
- [ ] Make one short test scan and one 360 photo. Open the scan's model preview and confirm an editable model export is available. Confirm the camera is recording **360**, if that camera supports it.
- [ ] Bring a tape or laser measure, portable light, lens cloth and this checklist. Have the water-map and fixture-plan images available offline.
- [ ] Use the existing asset label/ID in notes. If the match is uncertain, use a temporary observation name such as `OBS-001` and leave the asset match pending.

**Light:** prioritize exterior finishes, grass-area utilities and label photos before dark. If it is already dark, concentrate on well-lit utility clusters and accessible interiors; record an exterior daylight follow-up. LiDAR depth can remain useful in low light, but that does not guarantee readable labels, good textures or successful tracking in every app. Polycam distinguishes low-light LiDAR capture from image-based capture and advises deliberate, overlapping zones. [Official capture guidance](https://poly.cam/blog/how-to-scan-a-job-site-patterns-tips-and-pitfalls)

## At every utility cluster

- [ ] **Context:** a wide photo showing the cluster and an unmistakable unit corner, wall, curb or island; include a 360 stop where practical.
- [ ] **Identification:** a sharp, separately framed photo of each visible meter ID and each existing valve/box label. Review at full size before moving on; type the ID in the log exactly as shown.
- [ ] **Placement:** measure offsets to two identifiable fixed references where practical. Photograph and describe the measurement endpoints and units. A phone GPS pin is supporting context, not a precise survey coordinate.
- [ ] **Count:** record visible meters and shutoff boxes separately, plus obscured/unreadable items. Current references disagree: **24 mapped city meters / 28 workbook IDs**, plus **37 tenant shutoffs** shown as grouped counts. Do not turn a group count into invented individual identities.
- [ ] **Relationship:** keep “physically behind Unit 105” separate from “record says serves Unit 101.” Record visible labels and the basis for any claimed service relationship; uncertain links remain pending. This visit does **not** call for operating valves or interrupting service.
- [ ] **Condition:** capture visible damage, leaks or access obstructions with a detail photo and context photo. Record observations without diagnosing a hidden pipe route.

## LiDAR and 360 capture method

1. **Short, connected zones.** Walk slowly around nearby surfaces, turn gently, and keep fixed geometry in view. Overlap neighboring scans with the same wall corner, column and curb feature. Return to the starting landmark within a zone when practical, then inspect for doubled walls, bent columns or missing surfaces. Do not assume a loop automatically corrects drift.
2. **Keep geometry and appearance.** Choose a capture mode that produces a mesh or point cloud for model work. A Gaussian splat may be useful for presentation, but keep it separate from measured geometry. Capture full 360 stills at corners, utility groups and the ends of each walk, plus short route videos for context.
3. **Reference measurements.** In each zone record several identifiable control points; aim for three spread-out, non-collinear shared landmarks and independent lengths in different directions. Use measured column spacing, walkway width, box offsets or wall lengths. These are registration/check measurements, not surveyed control unless established as such.
4. **Check before leaving each zone.** Open the processed preview, check the known lengths, verify readable labels and re-capture failures immediately. Save the original before trimming or simplifying. Reflections, glass and featureless paving can leave gaps; capture another angle and note unresolved areas.

Apple describes its Measure results as approximate. Use a tape/laser reading with named endpoints for the important model checks; retain the phone measurement only with its method recorded. [Apple Measure guidance](https://support.apple.com/guide/iphone/measure-dimensions-iphd8ac2cfea/ios)

## Handoff — preserve originals, then export copies

Create `OTB_Capture_YYYY-MM-DD/` using the actual capture date, with `00_log`, `01_originals_insta360`, `02_originals_lidar`, `03_360_exports`, `04_model_exports`, `05_detail_photos` and `06_measurements` beneath it.

- **Insta360:** copy the complete original capture folders with their original filenames and companion files, including `.insv` / `.insp` when that camera produces them. Keep paired lens files together. Put descriptive names on the enclosing zone folder and exported copies, not on the camera originals. Export full-sphere, **2:1 equirectangular JPG** photos and 360 MP4 video when supported, retaining the original resolution you captured. [Insta360 spherical export documentation](https://faq.insta360.com/documents/Guide_to_Play_and_Edit_Spherical_Videos/)
- **LiDAR:** retain each native project and raw capture data if the app offers it. Export a textured **GLTF/GLB or OBJ** model if available; keep accompanying `.bin`, `.mtl` and texture files. Also export a **PLY/LAS** point cloud if available. **E57 is optional only if the chosen app actually exports it**; Polycam currently lists E57 as unsupported and restricts some export types by plan. Record the units, coordinate system if known, app and capture mode. [Polycam export matrix](https://learn.poly.cam/hc/en-us/articles/27756102599572-What-File-Types-Can-Polycam-Export)
- **Export naming:** `OTB_YYYYMMDD_Z05_OBS-014_meter-label_001.jpg` or `OTB_YYYYMMDD_Z01_scan-01.glb`. The log maps each export to its untouched original filename and permanent asset ID, if confirmed. A numbered source-plan symbol alone is not a permanent asset ID.
- **Backup:** keep originals on the devices until two copies open successfully. Bring the complete folders back; a public sharing link or screenshots alone do not preserve an editable scan.

### Minimal field log

For every scan/observation, record: **capture date/time + time zone · zone · original filename(s) · observation ID · permanent asset ID or pending · visible meter/fixture label · physical location · source-claimed served unit · photographs · measurement endpoints/value/units/method · lighting/access limits · observer · verification still needed**.

The capture does not automatically change an asset's verification status. After import, compare the new evidence with existing records, register the geometry, resolve identity/location differences and retain each original permanent asset ID.
