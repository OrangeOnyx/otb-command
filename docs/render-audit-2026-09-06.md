# Render audit — ChatGPT "geometry-faithful" site illustration (2026-09-06)

Operator supplied a ChatGPT-generated aerial-style rendering of OTB built from
A-1 geometry + satellite references via a "reference images → clean visual"
workflow. Graded against the architect CAD (`cad/Boulev_CLEAN.dxf`), the
recorded plat, and the frozen satellite base (`public/OTB-sat-base.jpg`).

**Verdict:** illustration grade only. Not usable as a lease exhibit, for
reserved-stall or monument-sign designation, or for common-area definition.
A-1 (CAD-traced, plat frame, REV 14) remains the exhibit source.

## Correct (inherited from A-1 geometry)
- L-shape massing; long building 101–133 backing Marie Antoinette, short
  building 135–149 along Patricia, 149 at the Arnould corner.
- Main-field module order: storefront row · aisle · double-loaded row with
  tree islands · aisle · double-loaded row · aisle · Arnould head-in row.
- JD Bank building at the Johnston × Arnould corner.
- 10-stall head-in row on Johnston south of unit 101 (REV 13 candidate row).
- Driveway A aligned with a median opening; rear Marie Antoinette bays.

## Wrong (material for lease/ops use)
1. **No property boundary.** Land east of Arnould is drawn as contiguous Belle
   parking with identical striping plus a second building. The neighbor is
   real but is not Belle property and is not laid out that way.
2. **Arnould median has three openings** and three Belle driveways. Reality:
   two segments, one 55' opening at Driveway A; Driveway B is at the bank
   notch and shared. The middle two cuts are invented.
3. **Frontage islands wrong.** Render shows teardrop end islands and a wide
   lawn. CAD: 3.1' planting strip, four curbed end caps, 9' strip at the
   7-space module, landscape (no stalls) along the 149 frontage. The render
   stripes stalls along 149's Arnould face.
4. **Patricia side.** A full striped row along the north face of the short
   building does not exist; Patricia is a 50' R/W with three service cuts
   and the 19-space Lot 8 pocket at the Marie Antoinette corner.
5. **Lot 7 misplaced** mid-block across Marie Antoinette; it sits directly
   across from Lot 8 at the Patricia end. Houses across M.A. are invented.
6. **JD Bank notch not distinguished** — no "NOT A PART", no parcel line,
   no reciprocal-servitude routing.
7. **Stall counts not preserved** (east modules show 14–15 stripes vs
   11/11/9/7). Nothing in the render ties to 314 or 324.
8. **Orientation unlabeled.** Plan is rotated ~38° from north (plat frame)
   with no north arrow or street names.

## Use policy
- Marketing/mood imagery: acceptable after removing the east-of-Arnould lot or
  masking it, and after fixing item 3 if the frontage is visible.
- Anything a tenant, lender, or the City could rely on: export from A-1.
- If regenerating, feed the A-1 SVG export as the primary reference and the
  satellite as secondary; the workflow's own rules classify generative output
  as a derived reference, never a precision trace.
