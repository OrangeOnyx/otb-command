Visible features used for the representative models:

- Bench: warm tan horizontal seat, warm tan top/side trim, dark decorative metal
  back infill, dark legs and armrests.
- Trash can: rectangular cabinet with warm tan vertical slats, dark frame, and a
  dark raised top with a disposal opening. The existing
  `src/assets/twin/storefront-reference.jpg` provides another view of this style.
- The benches shown face the parking area with their backs near storefronts.

These are presentation models informed by source photographs. Dimensions, exact
materials, manufacturer, and the appearance of every individual fixture remain
unverified. The reference photograph does not establish a present-day inspection
condition. Placement comes separately from the operator fixture plan and its
approximate registration to the native model.
