Tenant Architectural Signage Rendering System
Objective

Create a scalable AI-assisted production workflow that transforms tenant logos into premium architectural storefront signage renderings suitable for:

Leasing decks
Redevelopment presentations
Investor packages
Site plans
Broker marketing
Tenant merchandising packages
Master Architectural Signage Prompt
Create a photorealistic architectural storefront signage rendering using the uploaded tenant logo as the source artwork.

The final result should depict the logo transformed into premium fabricated exterior signage installed on a high-end shopping center or lifestyle center facade.

SIGN TYPE:
- Raised individual channel letters
- Dimensional fabricated signage
- Commercial-grade aluminum and acrylic construction
- Realistic side returns and mounting depth
- Slight beveling and edge detailing
- Flush-mounted or raceway-mounted depending on logo style
- Subtle premium illumination:
  - front-lit channel letters
  - halo-lit reverse channel letters
  - or a tasteful combination if architecturally appropriate

LOGO INTEGRITY:
- Preserve the exact tenant branding
- Preserve original typography
- Preserve exact logo proportions
- Preserve icon placement and spacing
- Preserve official brand colors
- Do not redesign the logo
- Do not add fake slogans or extra wording
- Do not distort or stylize the logo beyond realistic fabricated signage adaptation

ARCHITECTURAL STYLE:
- Upscale modern shopping center
- Lifestyle-center aesthetic
- Neutral luxury facade materials
- White stucco, limestone, concrete panel, wood accent, or charcoal metal facade
- Elegant storefront composition
- Professional commercial real estate appearance
- Clean architectural lines
- Minimal visual clutter

LIGHTING & REALISM:
- Realistic daylight or blue-hour exterior lighting
- Natural shadows beneath and behind letters
- Soft reflections on acrylic and metal surfaces
- Architectural realism similar to professional leasing brochure renderings
- Slight perspective angle as if photographed by a commercial real estate photographer
- Cinematic but believable lighting
- Clean premium environment

CAMERA STYLE:
- Eye-level architectural perspective
- Slightly angled storefront view
- Leasing brochure quality
- Ultra realistic
- High detail
- Sharp focus
- Balanced composition
- Exterior retail branding showcase

IMPORTANT:
The sign must look like a real fabricated tenant sign installed on an actual shopping center facade, not a flat graphic overlay or printed banner.

The rendering should feel suitable for:
- elite leasing presentations

- shopping center redevelopment packages
