## Message 3 — Assistant

**Scribe** is one of the few tools from that list that I think is genuinely useful for your workflow.

### What it does

Scribe watches what you do on your computer—clicks, typing, navigation—and automatically generates a step-by-step guide with screenshots.

Instead of spending 30 minutes documenting a process, you simply perform it once.

It creates documentation like:

1. Open AppFolio.
2. Navigate to Tenants → Leases.
3. Select Unit 145.
4. Click Edit Lease.
5. Update CAM charge.
6. Save changes.

Each step includes an annotated screenshot automatically.

### Where it fits for you

For **Orange Ocean** and **Belle Realty**, I'd use it to document:

- Tenant onboarding
- Lease renewal workflow
- Invoice creation
- Vendor payment process
- Insurance claim handling
- CAM reconciliation
- Creating work orders
- Bank deposit procedures
- Entering new tenants
- Updating rent rolls
- UniFi camera administration
- AI workflows you build

Then your paralegal or future employees can follow the SOP without you repeatedly explaining the process.

### Why it's valuable

You're trying to build systems that don't depend on you. Scribe captures your expertise while you're working, so documentation becomes almost effortless.

### One limitation

Scribe records **what** you clicked, but not necessarily **why**. For higher-quality SOPs, I recommend:

1. Record the workflow with Scribe.
2. Paste the generated document into ChatGPT.
3. Have ChatGPT rewrite it into a polished SOP with:
   - Purpose
   - Prerequisites
   - Step-by-step instructions
   - Decision points
   - Common mistakes
   - Quality-control checklist
   - Troubleshooting section

That combination produces documentation that's significantly better than either tool alone.

For your business, I would rate Scribe **8.5/10**. It's not flashy, but it's a practical way to capture operational knowledge and reduce dependence on tribal knowledge.
