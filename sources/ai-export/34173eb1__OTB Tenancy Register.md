## How this is built

Every instrument was read page by page as an image — the only method that sees the handwritten strikeouts these leases were amended by. Instruments are attached to a tenancy by the lease date they recite, never by the folder they were filed in, because three suites hold addenda belonging to leases that are not in the corpus at all. Only executed instruments move a value.
