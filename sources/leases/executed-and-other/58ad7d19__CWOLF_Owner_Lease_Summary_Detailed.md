## 6) Maintenance & Repairs (Risk Section)

### Tenant Responsibilities
- **HVAC:** Tenant pays **first $1,000** per repair visit AND **first $1,000** toward replacement cost
  - **Condition:** Tenant MUST maintain monthly maintenance contract with **Butcher Air Conditioning** for full term
  - **Credit:** If HVAC replacement occurs within 15 days of a preceding repair visit, tenant's prior $1,000 payment is credited toward the $1,000 replacement obligation
- **Interior Repairs:** All interior maintenance, repairs, and replacements (except roof, exterior walls, structural foundation)
- **Adjacent Areas:** Keep sidewalks and parking areas immediately adjacent to Premises free of trash/refuse
- **Waste Disposal:** Tenant responsible for contracting and paying for own trash collection (including dumpsters)
- **Utilities:** All utility payments (electric, gas, water, telephone) — see Section 11.01
- **General Condition:** Maintain Premises in good, safe, clean, healthful condition; comply with all laws/ordinances

### Landlord Responsibilities
- **Structural:** Roof, exterior walls, structural foundation
- **HVAC (partial):** Repair/replacement costs exceeding $1,000 per visit (tenant pays first $1,000)
- **Condition:** Landlord responsibility for HVAC contingent on tenant maintaining monthly Butcher Air Conditioning maintenance contract

### Risk Note
**HVAC cost-sharing is non-standard.** Tenant exposure is capped at $1,000/visit but requires ongoing monthly maintenance contract compliance. If tenant fails to maintain contract, Landlord's HVAC obligation may be voided.

## 9) Default / Remedies / Notice

### Events of Lessee Default (Section 18.01)
1. Adjudged bankrupt, files voluntary bankruptcy, makes general assignment for benefit of creditors, or receiver appointed
2. Fails to pay rent or any other amount due to Lessor
3. Fails to conduct business operations / demonstrates indicia of abandonment

- Terminate Agreement immediately

## 12) Recommendation

**Status:** ✅ **APPROVE** (Lease appears executed and effective November 1, 2024)

### Strengths
1. **Escalating Rent:** Base rent increases from $10.50/SF (stabilized) to $12.50/SF by final period — provides revenue growth
2. **NNN Structure:** CAM/Tax/Insurance passed through to tenant; Landlord insulated from operating cost increases (subject to 5% annual cap)
3. **Minimal TI Exposure:** "AS IS" lease; no landlord improvement obligations
4. **HVAC Cost-Sharing:** Tenant pays first $1,000/visit and must maintain monthly maintenance contract
5. **Standard Protections:** Insurance requirements, indemnity, assignment restrictions, default remedies all standard and landlord-favorable
6. **Use Restrictions:** Tenant acknowledges prohibited uses that protect other tenant exclusives

### Risks / Considerations
1. **Early Termination Right:** Tenant may exit with 15 days' notice during first 3 months (Jan-Mar 2025) — minimal landlord recourse
2. **6-Month Free Rent:** Base rent abatement through April 2025 reduces Year 1 revenue
3. **Renewal Rent TBD:** No formula for renewal rent; potential for negotiation friction in 2031
4. **HVAC Maintenance Contingency:** Landlord's HVAC obligation depends on tenant maintaining Butcher Air Conditioning contract — monitor compliance
5. **Missing Contact Info:** No email/phone for tenant in lease (obtain for DoorLoop and operational use)
6. **No Personal Guaranty:** Corporate tenant with no individual guarantor — credit risk depends on entity strength
