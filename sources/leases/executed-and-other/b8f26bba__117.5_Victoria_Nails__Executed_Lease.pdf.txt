FIRST ADDENDUM TO LEASE AGREEMENT 
 
 This First Addendum To Lease Agreement (the “ Addendum”) is entered into as of the 
dates set forth below, but effective for all purposes as of March 1, 2020 by and between BELLE 
REALTY OF LAFAYETTE, L.L.C., a Louisiana li mited liability company (the “ Lessor”), 
and THUY T. NGUYEN , an individual of the full age of majority domiciled in Lafayette, 
Louisiana (the “Lessee”). 
 
RECITALS 
 
WHEREAS, the Lessor and the Lessee  entered into a Lease Agreement effective March 
1, 2017, which provided for a thirty-six (36) month term (the “Lease Agreement”); and 
 
WHEREAS, the term of the Lease Agreement expires on February 29, 2020; and 
 
WHEREAS, the Lease Agreement contains an Option to Renew for one (1) additional 
thirty-six (36) month term, and the Lessee desires to exercise the Option to Renew; and 
 
WHEREAS, Lessor and Lessee wish to amend the Le ase Agreement to provide for the 
renewal of the Lease Agreement and to address certain other issues. 
 
AGREEMENT 
 
NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing the mutual covenants contained 
herein and other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which is hereby 
acknowledged, the parties hereto agree as follows: 
 
1. All capitalized terms used herein and not otherwise specif ically defined herein shall have 
the meanings ascribed to those terms in the Lease Agreement. 
 
2. In accordance with Section 31.01 of the Lease Agreement , the Lessee hereby exercises 
the Option to Renew, and desires to renew the Lease Agreement for a period o f thirty-six 
(36) months beginning March 1, 2020 and expiring February 28, 2023  (the “ Renewal 
Term”). 
 
3. Section 4.01(A) of the Lease Agreement is hereby amended to state that the Base Rent 
during the Renewal Term shall be $13.50  per square foot per year, pa yable monthly 
($1,990.13). 
 
4. In light of increasing taxes and i nsurance costs, Section 4.01(B)  of the Lease Agreement 
is hereby amended to read as follows:

Lessor, as additional rent (“Additional Rent”), the following amounts:

personal property taxes, improvement bonds and other charges or 
assessments which are levied upon or with respect to th e 
Boulevard Shopping Center Property, including any increase in

levied in connection with the Lessor ’s leasing or rental of the 
Boulevard Shopping Center Property, but shall not include any 
federal or state income tax imposed upon the Lessor.  Insurance 
charges shall include the cost of any insurance obtained by the 
Lessor in connection with the Boulevard Shopping Center 
Property, incl uding but not limited to fire, casualty and liability 
insurance policies.

day of the calendar month, a late fee in the fixed amount of 
$100.00 plus a daily charge beginning on the sixth day of the 
calendar month in the amount of $25.00 per day shall be charged 
and shall be due and payable by the Lessee for each late payment. 
 
All rental payments du e hereunder shall be made to the Lessor by 
mailing to P.O. Box 62230 , Lafayette, Loui siana 70596  or such

Section 31.01 - Option to Renew:  
Provided that Lessee is not in default in the performance of this Agreement, Lessee shall 
have the option to renew the lease for one (1) three (3) year term, commencing on the 
next day following the expiration of the Initial Term or renewal term, as the ca se ay be.

except that the Monthly Rental Amount shall be negotiated by the Lessor and the Lessee.

less than sixty (60) days prior to the expiration of the Initial Term.  If notice is not given 
in the manner provided herein and within the time specified, this option to renew shall 
terminate.
