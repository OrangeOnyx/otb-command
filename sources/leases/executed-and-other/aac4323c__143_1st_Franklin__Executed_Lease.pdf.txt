SECOND AMENDMENT TO LEASE
THIS SECOND AMENDMENT TO LEASE, made as of the 2t of Decer, 2025 by and
between Belle Realty of Lafayette, LLC (hereinafter referred to as "Landlord") and 1st Franklin Financial
Corporation, a Georgia Corporation (hereafter referred to as "Tenant"); as used herein "Landlord" and
"Tenant" shall include the successors and assigns of the respective parties, if any.
WITNESSETH:
WHEREAS, by Lease Agreement dated September 1, 2015, further amended and extended by an
addendum dated December 22, 2020, Landlord did lease and demise unto Tenant, certain Premises therein
more particularly described as 1,917 square feet of ground floor space located at 143 Arnould Boulevard,
Lafayette, LA 70508; and,
WHEREAS, the parties hereto desire to amend the Lease.
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the Premises and the sum of Ten and 00/100 ($10.00) Dollars
and other good and valuable consideration, in hand paid by Tenant to Landlord the receipt and sufficiency
of which is hereby acknowledged, it is mutually agreed as follows:
1. The term of the Lease is hereby extended for five (5) years commencing February 1, 2026, and
expiring January 31, 2031.
2. Base Rent for the extension term will be $2,556.00 per month for the entire term.
3. Tenant agrees to increase its pro rata share of additional rent to $798.75 per month commensurate
with the new lease term commencing February 1, 2026.
4. The Tenant may, by written notice sixty (60) or more days prior to expiration of the amended Lease
Term, extend the Lease for an additional term up to five (5) years upon the same terms and
conditions provided in this Lease commencing on the first day following the expiration of the
original Lease Term described in Paragraph 1. The monthly Base Rent for any extension or renewal
shall not exceed 10% of the current base monthly rental rate.
The signatories of this document each represent that they have been duly authorized to execute this
document.
In the event of a discrepancy between the Lease and this Amendment, this Amendment shall prevail. It is
mutually understood and agreed that the Lease shall be and remain in full force and effect, except as the
same is specifically modified and amended hereby. All covenants, terms, and obligations and conditions of
the Lease, not amended or modified hereby, are hereby ratified and confirmed.
This Agreement may be executed and delivered by the Parties in any number of counterparts, each of
which will be deemed an original and all of which together will constitute one and the same instrument. In
proving this Agreement in any judicial proceeding, it will not be necessary to produce or account for more
than one such counterpart signed by the Party against whom such enforcement is sought. Delivery of a
signature by facsimile transmission or by e-mail transmission of a document in the form of a portable
