FIRST ADDENDUM TO LEASE AGREEMENT 
 
 
 This First Addendum To Lease Agreement (the “ First Addendum”) is entered into as of 
the dates set forth below, but effective for all purposes as of the 1st of August 2020 by and between 
BELLE REALTY OF LAFAYETTE, L.L.C. , a Louisiana limited liability company (the 
“Lessor’), and J. C. KATE BOUTIQUE, LLC , a Louisian a limited liability company (the 
“Lessee”). 
 
RECITALS 
 
 WHEREAS, the Lessor and the Lessee entered into a certain Lease Agreement dated as 
of November 25, 2019, but effective for all purposes as of August 1, 2020 (the “Original Lease 
Agreement”); and 
 
 WHEREAS, the Lessor and the Lessee wish to amend the Original Lease Agreement to 
provide for a change in the agreed upon rental payments, to extend the Original Lease Agreement 
term, and to address certain other issues. 
 
 NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing mutual covenants contained 
herein and other good and valuable consideration, the rece ipt and sufficiency of which is hereby 
acknowledged by the parties, the parties hereto agree as follows: 
 
1. The Lessor and the Lessee hereby a gree that in accordance with and by further amending 
Section 3.01 of the Original Lease Agreement and subject to the terms and conditions set 
forth in this First Addendum, the Lessor and the Lessee are extending the Original Lease 
Agreement Initial Term, and such extension period shall be for an additional two (2) months 
such that the extended Initial Term of the Original Lease Agreement shall be for a total 
period of sixty -two (62) months and “shall commence on August 1, 2020 
(“Commencement Date ”) and shal l expire at midnight, Lafayette, Louisiana  time on 
September 30, 2025 (“Initial Term”)”; and  
 
2. The Premises Base Rent payable in accordance with Section 4.01(A) of the Original Lease 
Agreement shall be amended as follows: 
  
“Lease Months 1-2 $0 per square foot of the premises annually, payable monthly 
($0 per month) 
 
Lease Months 3-12 $13.00 per square foot of the premises annually , payable 
monthly ($2,758.17 per month) 
 
Lease Months 13-36 $13.50 per square foot of the premises annually, payable 
monthly ($2,864.25 per month) 
 
Lease Months 37-62 $14.00 per square foot of the premises annually, payable 
monthly ($2,970.33 per month)”

3. The Additional Rent payable in accordance with Section 4.01(B)(3) of the Original Lease 
Agreement shall be amended as follows: 
 
“The Total Monthly Rental Payment Amount for months one (1) through two (2) of the 
Initial Term, beginning August 1, 2020, through September 30, 2020, for the Premises 
shall be calculated as follows: 
 
   Base Rent      $ 0 
   Common Area Maintenance Charges   $ 445.55 
   Tax and Insurance Charges    $ 392.51 
 
   Total Monthly Rental Payment 
 (Months 1-2)        $ 838.06 
   
The Total Monthly Rental Payment Amount for months three (3) through twelve (12) 
of the Initial Term for the Premises shall be calculated as follows: 
 
   Base Rent      $ 2,758.17 
   Common Area Maintenance Charges   $ 445.55 
   Tax and Insurance Charges    $ 392.51 
 
   Total Monthly Rental Payment 
              (Months 3-12)                $ 3,596.23 
   
The Total Monthly Rental Payment Amount for months thirteen (13) though thirty-six 
(36) of the Initial Term for the Premises shall be calculated as follows: 
 
   Base Rent      $ 2,864.25 
   Common Area Maintenance Charges   $ 445.55 
   Tax and Insurance Charges    $ 392.51 
 
   Total Monthly Rental Payment 
              (Month 13-36)                $ 3,702.31 
 
The Total Monthly Rental Payment Amount for months thirty-seven (37) through 
sixty (62) of the Initial Term for the Premises shall be calculated as follows: 
 
Base Rent      $ 2,970.33 
   Common Area Maintenance Charges   $ 445.55 
   Tax and Insurance Charges    $ 392.51 
 
   Total Monthly Rental Payment    
                                    (Months 37-62)                $ 3,808.39”

4. Further, the third/final paragraph of Section 4.01(B)(4) of the Original Lease Agreement 
shall be amended as follows: 
 
“All rental payments due hereunder shall be made to the Lessor by mailing the same to 
P.O. Box, Lafayette, Louisiana 70506 or such other place, as the Lessor shall designate in 
writing.  Notwithstanding the foregoing, Lessor shall have the right to require at any time 
during the Initial Term that the Total Monthly Rental Payment and any other payments due 
to Lessor be paid to Lessor via electronic funds transfer, automatic wire transfer, ACH 
payment transfer, or other automated electronic payment method for deposit into and credit 
to a bank account designated in writing by Lessor.” 
 
5. The Lessor and the Lessee hereby agree that the terms and conditions of this First 
Addendum shall be kept confidential between the Lessor and Lessee and shall not be 
disclosed to any other persons, including but not limited to any tenants or occupants at On 
The Boulevard Shopping Center. The Lessee acknowledges that the foregoing covenant of 
confidentiality constitutes a portion of the consideration delivered to the Lessor to induc e 
the Lessor to execute and deliver this First Addendum. 
 
6. The remainder of the terms of the Original Lease Agreement not specifically amended 
herein by this First Addendum shall remain and be in full force and effect.
