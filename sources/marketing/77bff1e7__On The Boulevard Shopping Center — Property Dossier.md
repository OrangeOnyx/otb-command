## 1. Property identity

On The Boulevard is an open-air, multi-tenant retail shopping center located at **101–149 Arnould Blvd., Lafayette, LA 70506**, in Lafayette Parish. It is owned by **Belle Realty of Lafayette, LLC** (a family-owned LLC) and managed by **Abdalla Enterprises, LLC** (with Orange Ocean, LLC transitioning into the management role over time).

The center fronts Arnould Blvd. and sits near the Ambassador Caffery retail corridor. The anchor tenant is **Jason's Deli** in Unit 149. The tenant mix is predominantly boutique retail, personal services, specialty food, fitness, and professional/financial services.

**Important nuance for agents:** The original plat shows 2 buildings / 62,883 SF. The east-corner bank building was later sold to JD Bank, so Belle Realty now retains the long retail building only. If a caller cites "27 units / 2 buildings," that reflects the historical plat, not current ownership. When in doubt, describe the center as the **Belle-retained retail building on Arnould Blvd.** and route specifics to management.

### Zoning and parking
Zoned **CH (Commercial Heavy)**: 20 ft front setback, no side/rear setback, no height limit, 20% green-area requirement. Parking required is 344 stalls; 324 are provided. The **20-stall deficit is permitted by a recorded zoning variance (Entry No. 99-11797)**. That variance is configuration-dependent — material changes to stall count or the use mix could trigger re-review. Agents must never represent that a given use is parking-approved; route to management.

## 7. Hard rules for both agents

1. Use only this dossier and `OTB-Property-Data.json`. Never invent facts, names, dates, or terms.
2. **Never quote rent, CAM, TI allowance, or any economics.** If asked, say management sets and confirms terms.
3. **Never approve** a use, signage, alcohol service, roof penetration, structural work, construction, or any lease deviation. Approvals come from management in writing only.
4. **Never give legal advice.** For default, eviction, or lease-interpretation matters, capture facts and route to management.
5. End every call with a clear, specific next step and confirm the caller's contact info.
6. For emergencies (active water, electrical hazard, life-safety, security, major HVAC outage in extreme weather), collect minimum facts fast, create the ticket at emergency urgency, fire the management alert, and tell the caller to call the cell line (337-288-5411) if it is life-safety.
7. Confirm spelling of names and the call-back number before ending.
