Property Overview
A Legacy Center.
A Proven Operation.

On The Boulevard is a 62,749 SF open-air shopping center located at 101–149 Arnould Boulevard in Lafayette, Louisiana. Built in 1976 by the Abdalla family and comprehensively renovated in 2019, the center sits on a 4.84-acre site fronting Highway 167 (Johnston Street) — one of Lafayette's primary commercial corridors.

The center operates at 95% occupancy with 25 active tenants across fashion, food & beverage, health & wellness, services, and specialty retail. Two inline units — 131 and 133 — are currently available, representing a rare opportunity to join a fully stabilized, high-demand destination.

Ownership and management remain family-operated through Belle Realty of Lafayette, LLC, with day-to-day operations managed by Orange Ocean, LLC. The center has maintained an 88% tenant retention rate and carries a waiting list of qualified businesses seeking entry.

The Transformation Story

When the Abdalla family took active operational control of On The Boulevard in 2019, the center sat at approximately 50% occupancy. Through a comprehensive renovation program, strategic tenant curation, and hands-on property management, the center reached 95% occupancy — a transformation that produced a 32.9% increase in gross revenue and 22% appreciation in property value over the same period.

What This Means for Tenants

The 88% tenant retention rate is the most important number in this package. It reflects a management approach built on responsiveness, fair dealing, and a genuine interest in tenant success. The 14 businesses currently on the waiting list confirm that the center's reputation among local operators is strong. Joining On The Boulevard means joining a stable, well-run, community-anchored destination.
