## 1. Tenant notice (Belle Realty letterhead, LH2)

Send by the method each lease's notice clause requires (certified mail default), at least 30 days before the Effective Date.

---

[Date]

[Tenant Legal Name]  
[Suite No.], On The Boulevard Shopping Center  
[Tenant Notice Address per Lease]

**Re:  Notice of Change of Property Manager and Payment Instructions — On The Boulevard Shopping Center, 101-149 Arnould Blvd., Lafayette, Louisiana 70506**

Dear Tenant:

Belle Realty of Lafayette, LLC (“Landlord”), as owner of the above premises and Landlord under your lease dated [Lease Date] (the “Lease”), provides this notice pursuant to the notice provisions of the Lease.

Effective October 1, 2026 (the “Effective Date”), Landlord has appointed Cypress Command, LLC, a Louisiana limited liability company, as property manager and authorized agent for On The Boulevard Shopping Center. As of the Effective Date, the authority of any prior property manager to act on Landlord’s behalf under the Lease is withdrawn, and you should disregard any instructions received from any other party after that date.

**Payment of Rent and Other Charges**

All rent, additional rent, and other sums due under the Lease on or after the Effective Date shall be made payable to “Belle Realty of Lafayette, LLC” and remitted as follows:

- By check or ACH:  [Remittance address or ACH instructions — confirm by phone at the number below before changing any banking information]

- Through the tenant portal at [portal URL], once your login invitation is delivered

Payments received by any prior manager before the Effective Date will be credited to your account. Your rent amount, due date, security deposit, and all other Lease terms remain unchanged.

**Notices and Day-to-Day Contact**

Notices to Landlord under the Lease should continue to be sent to Belle Realty of Lafayette, LLC at the address above, with a copy to Cypress Command, LLC, 101-149 Arnould Blvd., Lafayette, LA 70506. For maintenance requests, insurance certificates, and operational questions, contact Cypress Command at 337-288-5411 or [tenants@cypresscommand.com].

Please update your records accordingly and, if your Lease requires a certificate of insurance naming the property manager, provide an updated certificate naming Cypress Command, LLC within thirty (30) days. Nothing in this notice amends or waives any provision of the Lease.

We appreciate your tenancy at On The Boulevard.

```
Sincerely,

BELLE REALTY OF LAFAYETTE, LLC
By its authorized property manager, Cypress Command, LLC


________________________________
Adam Anthony Abdalla
Authorized Representative
```

[Vendor Address]

## 4. Tenant phone/email FAQ (internal)

- What changed: the property manager. Cypress Command, LLC now manages the center for the owner, Belle Realty of Lafayette, LLC.
- What did not change: your lease, rent, due date, deposit, suite, and the people you deal with.
- Where to pay: payable to Belle Realty of Lafayette, LLC at the address in your notice; the portal invitation follows the week of October 1, 2026.
- Who to call: 337-288-5411 or tenants@cypresscommand.com.
- If asked why: "The owner engaged a new management company." Nothing further.
