Cypress Command · Visual System

Ink on paper. One surface, one accent, three signals.

One token set serves the company, the Playbook, Command Platform, and print. Terra is the only page-level accent. Cypress Green is a surface, never a color. Olive, Mustard and Oxblood carry state, never decoration. Color is never ornament: every colored element answers to a role, a state, a level, a chapter, or a risk.

Minimums: mark 20 px / 6 mm · lockup 120 px / 32 mm. The mark is monochrome and takes any single palette token by context — Ink on documents, Terra for bylines and covers, Paper on dark surfaces, Olive or Mustard when it labels a state, Cypress as a dark-on-light alternate. One color per instance; ≥4.5:1 against its ground; never Oxblood, never two-tone, never a gradient. Never rotate, shadow, outline, stretch, or set on photography without a panel. Inside the Playbook: Ink, Terra, Paper only.

10RulesA small set. Big discipline.
  1. Terra is the only page-level accent: eyebrows, active nav, links, byline mark, primary CTA.
  2. Cypress Green is a surface, never a text color. Text on it is Paper; accent on it is night Terra. It never appears in the Playbook. The mark may take it as a dark-on-light fill.
  3. Olive, Mustard, and Oxblood carry state. They never decorate.
  4. Oxblood means "do not." Never emphasis, never importance, never a level.
  5. Never more than one accent ink in view at once; Oxblood may join any of them as risk.
  6. Paper Deep is a surface, not a color. Objects inside a band return to Paper.
  7. No shadows. No radius above 2 px on documents, 4 px in product. Hairlines carry structure.
  8. Besley for display and long-form body; Archivo for UI and labels; Courier Prime for technical content only.
  9. Night inverts Paper and Ink and lifts the accents to their night values. Hierarchy and roles do not change.
  10. Orange Ocean, Moss, Amber, Charcoal, and Bone are retired and must not appear.
  11. Sub-brands are scopes of this token set, never new palettes.
  12. tokens.css is the single source of truth. Figma, Tailwind, print, and the Playbook consume it.
