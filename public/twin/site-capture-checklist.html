On The Boulevard / Capture 01

Find it. Identify it.
Give it a place in the twin.

Insta360 for visual context. iPhone/iPad LiDAR for nearby geometry. Clear labels and measured reference points connect both to permanent asset records.

First: use the remaining daylight. Capture grass-area utilities, exterior 360 views and legible labels before dark. If already dark, prioritize well-lit utility groups and interiors; log a daylight return for missing exterior detail.

Before starting

  • Charge devices; clean lenses; check storage.
  • Synchronize local date/time and note time zone.
  • Record camera, device, app/version and mode.
  • Test one 360 photo, one scan and a model export.
  • Bring tape/laser, portable light and offline source maps.

At every utility group

  • Context: wide photo + fixed landmark.
  • ID: readable close-up of every visible label.
  • Place: offsets to named corners/curbs.
  • Count: meters, shutoffs and unreadable boxes separately.
  • Evidence: location and served unit recorded separately. No valve operation is part of this capture.

On The Boulevard / Capture 02

Bring back usable evidence.

Scan method

  1. Small zones: divide by building side, utility group or room. Walk slowly around surfaces and turn gently.
  2. Shared landmarks: repeat the same wall corner, column and curb in neighboring scans. Return to the starting landmark where practical; check for drift.
  3. Measured checks: record shared control points and lengths in different directions, with endpoint photos and units.
  4. Preview onsite: check doubled walls, warped edges, holes and label readability. Re-capture failures before moving on.

Use an exportable mesh/point-cloud capture mode for geometry. A presentation splat is separate. Phone GPS and Apple Measure are approximate; record the measurement method.

Highest-value gaps

  • Water: 24 mapped meters vs 28 workbook IDs; 37 mapped shutoffs. Identify individual members without inventing matches.
  • Two Unit 113 meters: preserve W1252003 and W1202593 separately.
  • Columns: 39 on source plan vs 37 model objects. Check C25/C26 candidate correspondence and unmatched source 1/2.
  • Upper floors: 101 partial floor; 103 central opening. Measure actual heights/stairs.
  • Common areas: grass, curbs, service pads, drains, lighting bases and utilities outside the buildings.
